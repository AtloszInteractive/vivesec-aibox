Employee Handbook v4 - People and Workplace Policies

Working hours and flexibility. Standard hours are 40 per week. Employees may
adopt flexible start times between 07:00 and 10:00 by agreement with their lead.

Remote and hybrid work. The default is hybrid, with a minimum of two days per
week on site for most roles. Fully remote arrangements require manager approval
and a secure home-office checklist, including use of company devices only.

Leave. Annual paid leave is 25 working days, plus public holidays. Unused leave of
up to 5 days may be carried into the next year and must be used by 31 March. Sick
leave requires notification to the manager on the first day; a medical certificate
is required from the fourth consecutive day.

Onboarding. New joiners complete a 90-day onboarding plan. Week one covers
security training, device setup and access provisioning under least-privilege.
A 30-60-90 day review schedule applies, with a probation decision at day 90.

Performance and development. Reviews occur twice a year. Each employee has an
annual training budget and is encouraged to pursue one certification per year.

Code of conduct. The company maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment and
requires confidential handling of all customer data. Violations may lead to
disciplinary action up to termination.

Equipment. Laptops are refreshed every three years. Lost or stolen devices must be
reported to IT within 24 hours so they can be remotely wiped.
