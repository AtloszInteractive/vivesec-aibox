Information Security Audit Report - Q4 2025

Scope: Annual internal audit of the ViVeSec production environment against
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls and NIS2 readiness.

Overall posture: Strong. 92 of 100 sampled controls were effective. No critical
findings. Three medium findings and four low findings were identified.

Finding M-1 (Medium, A.8.16 Monitoring). Centralized log retention was 30 days;
policy requires 180 days for security-relevant events. Remediation: extend
retention and enable tamper-evident storage on the ViVeSec Box. Owner: SecOps.
Due: 31 January 2026.

Finding M-2 (Medium, A.5.30 ICT readiness for business continuity). The disaster
recovery runbook had not been tested in the last 12 months. Remediation: perform
a full failover test and document RTO/RPO. Target RTO is under 60 minutes.

Finding M-3 (Medium, A.8.24 Use of cryptography). Two internal services still used
TLS 1.1. Remediation: enforce TLS 1.2+ and add post-quantum key exchange on the
backup channel.

Low findings concerned password rotation reminders, an outdated asset inventory
entry, missing MFA on one legacy admin account, and incomplete vendor due
diligence records.

Conclusion: The environment is audit-ready for certification with the medium
findings remediated. The immutable, isolated backup design was highlighted as a
key strength against ransomware.
