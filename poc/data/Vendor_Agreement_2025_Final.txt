Vendor Agreement and Service Contract 2025 - Final

Parties: ViVeSec Kft. (Client) and NimbusCloud Services GmbH (Vendor).
Effective date: 1 January 2025. Initial term: 24 months with automatic renewal
for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives written notice of
non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

Payment terms: The Client shall pay invoices within 30 days of receipt. Late
payments accrue interest at 0.05% per day. Fees are fixed for the initial term
and may increase by no more than 3% per renewal period.

Service levels: The Vendor guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime as defined in the
linked SLA. Service credits of 5% of the monthly fee apply for each 0.1% below
target, capped at 25% of the monthly fee.

Liability: Each party's aggregate liability is limited to the total fees paid in
the 12 months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or
consequential damages. This cap does not apply to breaches of confidentiality or
data protection obligations.

Termination: The Client may terminate for convenience with 60 days notice. Either
party may terminate for material breach not cured within 30 days of written
notice. On termination the Vendor must return or securely delete all Client data
within 15 days and certify deletion in writing.

Confidentiality and data protection: The Vendor processes personal data strictly
as a processor under GDPR Article 28, only on documented instructions, and must
notify the Client of any data breach within 24 hours.
