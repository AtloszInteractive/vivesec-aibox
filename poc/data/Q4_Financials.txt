Q4 2025 Financial Summary - Management Accounts

Revenue for Q4 2025 was EUR 4.82 million, up 18% year over year and 4% ahead of
the plan of EUR 4.63 million. Full-year revenue reached EUR 17.6 million.

Gross margin improved to 71%, from 67% in Q4 2024, driven by a higher share of
recurring software revenue and lower third-party hosting costs after the move to
on-premise ViVeSec infrastructure.

Operating expenses were EUR 2.31 million, slightly above plan due to additional
R&D hiring in the AI team. EBITDA was EUR 1.12 million, an EBITDA margin of 23%,
compared with 19% a year earlier.

Cash flow: Operating cash flow was EUR 0.94 million. Free cash flow after capital
expenditure of EUR 0.21 million (primarily edge AI hardware for pilots) was
EUR 0.73 million. Closing cash balance was EUR 6.4 million with no debt.

Key KPIs: Net revenue retention 112%, gross churn 4.1%, CAC payback 11 months,
ARR EUR 15.2 million. Deferred revenue grew to EUR 3.1 million.

Variance commentary: The main positive variance versus plan was faster expansion
within existing enterprise accounts. The main negative variance was higher
personnel cost, a deliberate investment to accelerate the AI Box roadmap.

Outlook: Management reaffirms full-year 2026 guidance of 20-25% revenue growth
and an EBITDA margin above 24%.
