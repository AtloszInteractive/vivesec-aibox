Cloud Infrastructure SLA 2026 - Service Level Agreement

§2 Service Levels & Availability

2.1 Uptime Commitment — Provider guarantees a monthly availability of 99.9% measured against the production control-plane endpoints.

2.2 Downtime Indemnification — Should monthly availability drop below 99.9%, Customer may submit a service credit claim. Claims must be filed manually within seven (7) business days of the underlying incident or the right to credit is waived.

2.3 Credit Cap — Aggregate service credits shall not exceed twenty percent (20%) of the monthly subscription fee for the affected billing period.

§11 Data Protection & Cryptographic Controls

11.1 Encryption at Rest — All Customer Data is encrypted with AES-256 keys provisioned per tenant within Provider's hardware security modules.

11.2 Key Rotation — Encryption keys must be rotated on a bi-annual cadence; Customer is responsible for initiating coordinated rotation windows and validating successful re-wrap of stored objects.

11.3 Key Custody — Customer may opt into Hold-Your-Own-Key mode at any time with thirty (30) days notice to Provider's Trust Operations team.

§17 Invoicing & Payment Terms

17.1 Billing Cycle — Provider invoices Customer on the first business day of each calendar quarter for the preceding consumption window.

17.2 Payment Terms — Net-45 from invoice date. Customer's Finance team is expected to perform a quarterly invoicing verification against metered usage records before settlement.

17.3 Disputes — Billing disputes must be raised in writing within fifteen (15) business days of invoice receipt.
