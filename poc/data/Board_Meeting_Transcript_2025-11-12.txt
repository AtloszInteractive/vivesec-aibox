Board Meeting Transcript - 12 November 2025

Attendees: Chair, CEO, CFO, CTO, Head of AI, two non-executive directors.

1. AI Box strategy. The CTO presented the plan to ship the ViVeSec AI Box on the
NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano 8GB Super as the entry hardware. The board agreed that a
fully offline, retrieval-augmented assistant running a small quantized model is
the right first product. Decision: approve the entry-tier hardware direction.
Action item: Head of AI to deliver an on-device benchmark report by 5 December
(owner: Head of AI).

2. Roadmap and modules. The board reviewed the module plan: Legal Ops, Finance
Assistant, Compliance Officer and Workforce/HR. Decision: prioritize Legal and
Finance for the MVP. Action item: Product to produce module specs by 20 November
(owner: Head of Product).

3. Risks. Two risks were escalated. First, dependency on a single hardware vendor;
mitigation is to validate a second module tier for larger deployments. Second,
the perception that an offline box cannot generate, only search. The board asked
for a clear demonstration of generative answers on the entry device. Action item:
prepare a proof-of-concept demo for the January review (owner: CTO).

4. Finance. The CFO confirmed Q4 revenue of EUR 4.82 million, ahead of plan, and
reaffirmed 2026 guidance. No objections.

5. Next meeting: 14 January 2026. The Chair closed the meeting.
