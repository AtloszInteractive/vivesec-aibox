Master Services Agreement 2025 — Summary

This agreement is between ViVeSec Kft. (the Provider) and the Client.
The initial term is 24 months, renewing automatically for 12-month periods.
Either party may terminate for convenience with 60 days written notice.
Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within 30 days.
On termination the Provider must return or securely delete all Client data
within 15 days and certify the deletion in writing.
The Provider processes personal data strictly as a processor under GDPR
Article 28 and must notify the Client of any data breach within 24 hours.
