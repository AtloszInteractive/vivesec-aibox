Budget Memo — Legal (synthetic)

Author: Balazs Nemeth

The Legal department budget cap for the fiscal year is 4150 thousand EUR. Discretionary spending must be reduced by 18 percent compared to the previous year. Requests above the cap require CFO approval.

All Legal budget submissions are due by 2026-01-08. Late submissions roll over to the next review cycle. Travel and training budgets are tracked separately.
