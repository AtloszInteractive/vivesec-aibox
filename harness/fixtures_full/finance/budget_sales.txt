Budget Memo — Sales (synthetic)

Author: Tamas Olah

The Sales department budget cap for the fiscal year is 4700 thousand EUR. Discretionary spending must be reduced by 8 percent compared to the previous year. Requests above the cap require CFO approval.

All Sales budget submissions are due by 2026-11-06. Late submissions roll over to the next review cycle. Travel and training budgets are tracked separately.
